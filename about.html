About This Portal

This portal supports United Nations SDG 4 (Quality Education) by giving parents, teachers, and villagers a direct digital voice to ensure government schools have basic human facilities.

Why Direct Reporting?

  • No middlemen: Complaints reach education directors without getting blocked or altered.
  • Photographic proof: Visual evidence ensures authorities cannot ignore infrastructural negligence.
  • Full transparency: Every complaint gets a permanent tracking number.
  • Safety guaranteed: Anonymous reporting protects villagers from local harassment.
