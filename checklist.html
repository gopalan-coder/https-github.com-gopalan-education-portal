Mandatory School Facilities Checklist

According to RTE and national educational standards, every school must provide these 8 basic facilities. If any are missing, report immediately.

🏫

Enough classrooms and teachers

Adequate teacher-student ratio (1:30) and weatherproof classrooms.

💧

Clean drinking water

Safe, tested potable drinking water tap or RO purification.

🚻

Separate toilets for girls and boys

Functional, hygienic, and secure sanitation with running water.

💡

Electricity, desks and books

Working lights, fans, benches, desks, and free textbook supply.

🧱

Safe building and boundary wall

Structural safety certificate, non-leaking roofs, secure gates.

⚽

Playground & sports equipment

Open recreational ground and basic physical education gear.

♿

Access for children with disabilities

Barrier-free access ramps, handrails, and accessible toilets.

🍲

Mid-day meal programme

Hygienic kitchen, nutritious hot meal served daily on time.
